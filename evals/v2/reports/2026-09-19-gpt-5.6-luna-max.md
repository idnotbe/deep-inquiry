No P0/P1 defect was found in the author's static local dataset/runner review or the two read-only report-review rounds. This is not a claim that the benchmark is free of all defects outside the reviewed scope.

Known limitations that remain:

- Codex did not return an authenticated actual model snapshot before failing.
- The current adapter does not establish a clean global-profile baseline.
- The current adapter does not prove the requested model or effort was the effective runtime setting; the resume gate requires host evidence for model, fallback and supported effort.

- Native trigger/loading telemetry and the multi-turn driver remain unavailable in this revision.
- No qualitative independent review was possible because there were no model outputs to review.
- The cases in this public suite are not secret holdouts. No generalization claim should be made without private final cases.
- Raw trace/stderr capture can contain host metadata; future runs require a disposable non-production profile and scrubbed retention before any artifact is published.
