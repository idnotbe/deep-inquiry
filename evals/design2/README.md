# Design 2: baseline screening excluded this family

The D2C native baseline screen is complete. All three actual answers chose C plus one fallback case and reached eight customer confirmations for $19. Two independent graders both marked **3/3 full passes**; their scores were 10/10/9 and 10/10/10. The unchanged exclusion rule therefore removes this family as too easy for the difficult core. See the [screening report](reports/2026-10-03-screening.md), [exact final responses](reports/responses.json), [both independent grades](reports/grades.json), and [redacted execution evidence](reports/evidence.json). No scored treatment, D2H confirmation, or D2K control turn ran. No skill-effect or effective-backend claim follows.

This is one prospective, screening-only candidate for the `participant_adaptation_observation` family described in [the hard-evaluation design](../../docs/2026-10-01-hard-eval-design.md). D2C is the calibration case. D2H is a different, writer-seen confirmation instance reserved for use only if this family is validly hard and a qualifying core later exists. D2K is a separate conventional-winning control. All three are self-contained; no external facts or tools are needed to answer them.

The decision mechanism is provider selection over complete registered demand. Confirmation-only payment does not make the accepted-case dashboard a measure of all eligible outcomes. The independent intake record keeps refused cases visible; limited fallback can turn some of those refusals into confirmed outcomes within a hard budget. The control changes the fallback constraint so keeping the simple contract is the direct winner. This is distinct from retired C04 rate-floor/all-or-nothing bonus calculations and the later C04 audit/recurrence setting. Private oracles in `temp/2026-10-03-design2/oracles.md` document feasible witnesses and comparison checks; they are never model input.

The [rubrics](rubrics.json) have five task-specific 0/1/2 dimensions for each case. A full pass is at least 8/10 without a critical failure. A brief correct recommendation can receive full credit, and valid equivalent reasoning is accepted. Do not score style, phrase matching, unnecessary alternatives, or private reasoning. Score only actual final answers.

The [protocol](protocol.json) preserves the historical pretrial plan and `NOT_RUN` text; its bytes were frozen before screening and were not rewritten to match the result. The fixed rule excludes a family with at least two full passes in three baseline trials or a normalized mean strictly greater than 0.70. Both graders independently reach exclusion here. One family also cannot meet the unchanged six-family difficult-core minimum. D2H and D2K remain unexecuted, so the planned eleven-output ceiling was not used.

Independent construct and oracle reviews preceded the exact-byte freeze. A final native delivery canary passed before screening. The requested/configured host model was `gpt-6-sol` at medium effort, without an external API, but effective backend and effort remain unverified. The three frozen case/rubric/protocol inputs and skill package remain unchanged. The final runner repair and verification are described in the report; the protocol is a historical pretrial document, not a current status record.
