# Resolve findings and revise

Read when issues remain, an existing candidate must change, future conditions matter, or a selected premise collapses. The root is the sole source of counters, severity definitions and approval gates.

## Assign the next action

Split issues with multiple causes and resolve prerequisite information or decisions first. Prioritize P0, P1 and active blockers while respecting agreed business priorities. Do not send every issue to another review.

| Issue | Next action |
|---|---|
| Design, writing or calculation defect within a valid direction | M, affected verification, integrated review and reclassification. |
| Unverified fact, capability, number or effect | E; incorporate the evidence and use M only if a change is needed. |
| Competing unresolved causes | H and discriminating E; choose a remedy only when sufficiently distinguished. |
| User-owned interpretation, preference, risk or authority | Q, end the response, incorporate the reply, resume the paused issue. |
| Future-dependent merit | Define a permitted default and observable switching policy, or expose the unresolved decision. |
| Verified collapse of the selected core premise | Local M if sufficient; otherwise the root's re-exploration gate, A, then M. |
| Conflicting hard requirements | Present conflict evidence and required change through Q; do not relax either silently. |
| Lower-severity improvement | M if needed for agreed quality; otherwise retain material residuals and use F. |
| No material defect or acceptance-blocking unknown | F. |

## Handle uncertainty without pretending it is solved

For a conditional strategy, identify the observation, decision owner, decision time, alternative action, switching cost/delay/authority and return conditions. If observing or switching is infeasible, do not sell the condition as an executable plan. Present a robust permitted default or the user's unresolved choice.

For premise collapse, verify the evidence before reopening the core approach. Preserve unaffected decisions. A local fix cannot rescue a disproven structure merely by being smaller. Conversely, an appealing idea without action-changing evidence does not justify replacing a functioning baseline. Required approval comes before reopening, and the counters cannot be bypassed through a new label, subtask or lens.

## Perform one controlled revision

Check remaining revision capacity and increment the counter before starting, as defined in the root. Batch confirmed same-cause/related-area issues into one next candidate version. Keep one writer per affected area and reconcile independent changes through the single current candidate.

Prefer a targeted edit when it preserves the same behavior as a rewrite; inspect the actual diff for unrelated changes. Add regression tests in the existing test structure when they protect behavior; keep disposable experiments in an authorized scratch area and remove only those you created. Report unrelated defects rather than fixing them without authorization.

Link each issue to its minimal sufficient change and observable closure evidence. Recheck affected criteria, scenarios and dependencies; a writer's claim of success is insufficient. Finish the round after verification. Resume a paused round without incrementing again.

If a revision worsens the result, revert to a better version while preserving spent rounds and unresolved issues. Revalidate evidence for the restored version. Return to integrated review without repeating unchanged full reviews absent new evidence. Stop when the next move needs user authority, missing facts or an exhausted limit, and distinguish a blocked candidate from an accepted one.
